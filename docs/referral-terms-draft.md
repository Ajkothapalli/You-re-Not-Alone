# Referral terms — DRAFT

**Not published. Not legal advice.** This is a draft for the in-app policy
screen and the website terms page, for review before either goes live.

Two audiences, one meaning. The in-app version is the one a real person reads
at the moment they are deciding whether to invite someone they care about, so
it is written plainly and says the uncomfortable parts out loud rather than
burying them. The website version is the same promises in the register the
rest of that page uses.

---

## In-app policy screen

### Giving someone 7 days

When you share soulyap, the person who installs it from your link gets **7
days of unlimited reading**, free. It starts as soon as they open the app.

Once they've settled in — read a little, written something of their own — a
**thank-you week** arrives for you too. It isn't instant, and it isn't
guaranteed: it depends on them, not on you, and we'd rather tell you that now
than let you wonder later.

### What we never tell you

We will never tell you what the person you invited read, or wrote, or when.
You won't see their name, their confessions, or any activity. If a thank-you
week arrives, that's all you'll learn — that one arrived.

This isn't us being coy. Anonymity here only works if it holds in every
direction, including from the person who brought you in.

### The limits

- **Four thank-you weeks per 30 days.** Past that we'll say thank you, but
  the week doesn't stack up or carry over.
- **Free reading has no cash value.** It can't be sold, transferred, refunded
  or exchanged for money or anything else.
- **One gift per person and per device.** Making extra accounts to collect
  weeks doesn't work, and trying it forfeits rewards on every account
  involved.
- **We can change or end this at any time.** If we do, weeks already granted
  stay yours for the time they have left.

### If something goes wrong

If you think a week should have arrived and didn't, write to us. We can look
into it, and we can fix it — without either of us learning anything about the
other person.

## Website terms — referral section

**Referral programme.** Users who install soulyap via a shared link may
receive seven (7) days of complimentary premium access. The user whose link
was used may receive a further seven (7) days once the invited user has
established genuine use of the service, as determined by soulyap.

Complimentary access has **no cash value** and is not transferable,
refundable, or exchangeable for money or other consideration. It is not a
security, a credit, or a store of value.

Rewards are limited to **four (4) per referring user per rolling 30-day
period**. Referrals beyond that limit are recorded but not rewarded, and are
not carried forward.

Rewards are granted per person and per device. Creating multiple accounts,
using multiple devices, or otherwise attempting to obtain rewards to which a
user is not entitled **forfeits all rewards** on every account involved and
may result in termination of access.

soulyap **may modify, suspend, or discontinue** the referral programme at any
time, with or without notice. Complimentary access already granted will remain
valid for its remaining term.

**Privacy.** Referring users receive no information about the users they
invite. No identity, content, or activity is disclosed in either direction at
any time.

## Open questions for the lawyer

1. **Is "no cash value" enough** for Indian gift-voucher / prepaid-instrument
   rules, given the reward is service access rather than stored value?
2. **The forfeiture clause** — can rewards be revoked on an account after the
   fact, and does that need its own notice?
3. **"as determined by soulyap"** is deliberately vague in the website text
   because publishing the exact criteria is an invitation to game them. Is
   that acceptable, or does consumer law require the conditions be stated?
4. **Play Store policy** — confirm a referral that grants premium access does
   not count as an incentivised install under current Play rules.
5. **DPDP** — the device fingerprint used for fraud prevention is derived from
   IP and user agent. Confirm the lawful basis and whether it needs naming in
   the privacy policy.
